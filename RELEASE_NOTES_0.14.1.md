# FabOS Alpha 0.14.1 — Scroll & Status Polish

- Action Center is vertically scrollable and no longer stops at 12 items.
- Notifications are vertically scrollable and can display up to 100 unread items.
- Mouse-wheel scrolling works while hovering custom scrolling panels.
- Dashboard Live Printers and Activity are scrollable.
- Activity Journal has vertical and horizontal scrollbars.
- Global Search results have vertical and horizontal scrollbars.
- System Health has vertical and horizontal scrollbars.
- The sidebar no longer says `Ready • 1 warning`.
- Warning state now reads `⚠ 1 warning — click for details`.
- Clicking the sidebar health indicator opens Backup & Health immediately.
