# FabOS Alpha 0.6.1 — Quote Archive

## Changes

- Quotes page now has Active Quotes and Quote History tabs.
- Active Quotes shows only Draft and Sent quotes.
- Quote History shows Approved, Declined and Expired quotes.
- Approved quotes disappear from the active list immediately after conversion.
- Converted quotes remain permanently available in history and keep their order link.
- Status filters change automatically for the selected tab.
- Quote rows are color-coded by status.
- Search and sortable columns work independently in both views.

No quote or order records are deleted.
